# Shops, Crafting & Economy — English

1. Download the optional editable Lumen Vale example, open `project.godot` in Godot 4.7.2 and press F5. Choose English, then open Shops, Crafting & Economy. To run the component scene directly, open `demo/economy_quick_start.tscn` and press F6. The required addon-only download contains no example project.
2. Open Craft, choose Potion, leave Batches at 1 and choose Craft. Two of the starting 12 herbs become one potion. Bag shows the result. In Shop, choose an item and quantity, then Buy or Sell; the preview explains price or why the action is unavailable.
3. In Save & Load, choose Save economy. Close and reopen the example, then choose Load economy. The example uses `user://economy-workshop.json`; loading replaces this economy session. Quests, health and world position need your game's own save system.
4. To install only the addon, copy `addons/fableweld_inventory` into your project and enable Fableweld Shops, Crafting & Economy in Project Settings > Plugins. Keep authored catalogs and generated scenes outside `addons`. Validate the catalog before saving; use Setup to generate an economy scene at an unused `res://` path.
5. Before updating, preserve your project and saves. Disable the plugin, close Godot, replace only the vendor addon folder, reopen and validate a copy of your catalog/save. Keep stable item and session IDs. For a legacy Inventory save, preview the upgrade and write a different economy file; the original remains unchanged. Combined clock saves are not supported by this upgrade.
6. Core trading and crafting need no clock addon. The language selector translates built-in controls; optional item dictionaries supply authored translations. Missing translations use original text. In generated Vendor practice, click or Tab to the practice area before moving with arrow keys; trading controls retain focus.
