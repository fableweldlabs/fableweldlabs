# Characters, Stats & Abilities 1.1.0

Character/class/stat/ability/status authoring, party progression and reusable roster/summary UI for Godot.

The addon is the only required download. The editable Lumen Vale world and Windows demo are optional.

## Scope

Verified on Godot 4.7.2 Windows. No skill-tree editor or combat resolution. Your game owns targeting, cost deduction, status application/ticking and save-file storage. Built-in translations are AI-assisted; authored IDs and text are preserved.
