# Calendar & Growth Pro

[Product and downloads](https://fableweld-labs.itch.io/calendar-growth-pro) — $19.99 USD or more. Current release: **2.1.0**.

Custom calendars, independent clocks, growth stages and coordinated saves for registered participants in Godot. Includes the shared Time & Seasons foundation, guided tools, clock/calendar displays, sleep and speed controls, and optional atmosphere workflows.

## Downloads

- **Addon Only (Required):** install `addons/rpg_time` in your own project.
- **Editable Project (Optional):** separate Lumen Vale Pro example.
- **Windows Demo (Optional, Free):** try the example without Godot; extract the whole ZIP and keep its EXE and PCK together.

The example is not needed to use the addon. Tested target: Godot 4.7.2 on Windows x64. Other engine versions and platforms are not claimed.

## Upgrading

Back up your project and saves first. Install only one edition at a time. Keep your own profiles and generated scenes outside the replaceable addon directory. Read the packaged installation and migration guides before replacing Lite or Starter. Version 2.1.0 aligns the shared clock core and edition versions, with exact fractional clock save/restore and validated Lite/Starter upgrades.

## Scope and licensing

Registered-state saves cover configured clocks and growth participants, not your entire game. Schedule diagnostics have documented bounds. Atmosphere depends on the renderer. This product does not include quests, inventory, combat, weather simulation or NPC pathfinding.
