# Inventory & Equipment for Godot

[Product, downloads and free Windows demo](https://fableweld-labs.itch.io/inventory-equipment) — **1.1.0, $4.99 USD or more**.

Build item catalogs, slot-backed bags, equipment, hotbars, containers and inventory-session saves. The authoring dock and reusable panels support twelve languages. The addon does not require a Calendar or NPC product.

Download **Addon Only** for your own project. The editable Lumen Vale example and standalone Windows demo are separate optional downloads. The demo is free; it does not grant redistribution rights to the paid Inventory source.

## Boundaries

Verified target: Godot 4.7.2, Windows x64. Other targets are unverified. Translations are AI-assisted; independent native-speaker or physical-DPI certification is not claimed.

Inventory saves cover registered bags, equipment and hotbars, not the entire game. Use one writer per save path; supported files are limited to 1 MiB. Your game supplies item effects and base character stats. Multi-cell packing, per-instance durability, a two-handed equipment contract and multiplayer authority are not included. Basic shop/recipe resource APIs are present; a complete crafting/economy controller is a separate product.
